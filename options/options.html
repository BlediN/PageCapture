Pro features

Evidence mode Pro Every export gets a band with the page address, the exact time with its time zone, and a SHA-256 checksum of the pixels; the same facts are written into the file. Images are saved as PNG and PDF pages losslessly, so the checksum can be verified later at fullpagecapture.net/verify. Replaces the PDF address bar.
Searchable PDF Pro PDF exports carry the words of the page over the picture, so the text can be selected, searched and copied. Nothing is visible on the page itself.
Batch capture Pro A list of addresses captured in one go, on its own page. Open it from here or from your captures.
Open batch capture
Where captures go Pro One at a time. Captures already sent stay where they are.
Save to the cloud Pro Send a capture straight to a "FullPage Capture" folder in your own cloud storage.
